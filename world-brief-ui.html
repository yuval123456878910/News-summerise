World Brief, the screen before we build it

This prototype is clickable. Switch English and Hebrew, change the time range (Today, 2 days, Week), tick countries, and open the AI provider settings from the top bar. All headlines are sample content. Tell me what to change before I start coding.

What to look at

  1. 1
    AI provider buttonShows which AI is in use and if its key works. Opens the settings screen.
  2. 2
    English / עבריתSwitches the whole app to Hebrew, right-to-left, and the AI writes summaries in Hebrew too.
  3. 3
    CountriesSearch and tick as many as you want. The number is how many stories were found.
  4. 4
    Time rangeToday, 2 days (grouped Today / Yesterday) or Week (top stories plus a day-by-day timeline).
  5. 5
    AI summaryOne short summary across all ticked countries, with the model name under it.
  6. 6
    Story actions"Ask about this" sends the story to the chat. "Translate" flips one story between English and Hebrew.
  7. 7
    ChatAsk anything about the news. Answers show their sources and each has a Translate button.
  8. 8
    SettingsOne card per provider: OpenRouter, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini. Paste a key, test it, pick a model.

On a phone the brief and the chat become two tabs at the bottom of the screen.
